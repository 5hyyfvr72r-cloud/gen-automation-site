Process

How it works

From first call to a running system, with clear timelines and updates the whole way. You always know what’s being built and why.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A free 30-minute call about how your business runs today: where leads come from, where time goes, and what’s slowing you down. If automation isn’t the answer, we’ll tell you.

    You get: a straight answer on whether it’s worth building, and where to start.

  2. 02

    Design

    We map your market or your workflow step by step, choose the tools, and decide where a person should stay in the loop.

    You get: a written plan with scope, timeline and a flat project price.

  3. 03

    Build and deploy

    We build the system inside the tools you already use, test it on real data, and get it running. You get updates as it comes together.

    You get: a working system, an SOP, and a walkthrough video.

  4. 04

    Optimize

    We measure what it’s doing, tune it, and hand it off finished. It’s yours to run.

    You get: a finished system you own, with the option of ongoing support.

Step one takes 30 minutes.

Free, no pressure. You’ll leave knowing whether it’s worth building.
